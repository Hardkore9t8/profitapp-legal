Privacy Policy for ProfitApp

Last Updated: January 22, 2026

Quick Summary: We collect location data only during job tracking, use Firebase for secure data storage, and never sell your personal information. You can delete your data at any time.

Introduction

Welcome to ProfitApp ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ProfitApp (the "App").

By using ProfitApp, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

Account Information

  • Email address (for account creation and login)
  • Name (optional, for personalization)
  • Password (encrypted and securely stored)

Job and Earnings Data

  • Job details (platform, earnings, distance, time)
  • Expense information (fuel, maintenance, tolls)
  • CSV files you import (Uber, DoorDash, Instacart, etc.)
  • Receipt images and expense documentation
  • Notes and custom job information

User Preferences

  • App settings and preferences
  • Notification preferences
  • Goal tracking information
  • Custom categories and tags

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

Location Data

  • GPS coordinates during job tracking
  • Route information and mileage
  • Start and end locations for jobs
  • Background location (only when actively tracking a job)

Device Information

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • App version and build number

Usage Data

  • Features used within the App
  • Time spent in the App
  • Interaction patterns
  • Error logs and crash reports

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

Authentication Providers

  • Google Sign-In: Email, name, profile picture
  • Apple Sign In: Email, name (if provided)

Firebase Services

  • Authentication data
  • Cloud storage metadata
  • Analytics data (if enabled)

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Core App Functionality

  • Job Tracking: Record and track your gig work jobs with GPS-enabled mileage logging
  • Profitability Calculations: Calculate earnings, expenses, and profit margins
  • Analytics: Generate reports and insights about your gig work performance
  • Data Sync: Synchronize your data across devices using Firebase
  • CSV Import: Process and import earnings data from gig platforms

2.2 App Improvement

  • Performance Monitoring: Identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • Feature Development: Understand which features are most valuable
  • User Experience: Improve app design and usability

2.3 Communication

  • Account Management: Send important account-related notifications
  • App Updates: Notify you of new features and improvements
  • Support: Respond to your questions and support requests

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

3.1 Service Providers

We use third-party services to operate the App:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) - Authentication, database, cloud storage, analytics
    Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Apple Services - App distribution, Sign in with Apple
    Apple Privacy Policy

3.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to:

  • Court orders or legal processes
  • Government or regulatory requests
  • Protection of our rights, property, or safety
  • Prevention of fraud or illegal activity

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Firebase Cloud Firestore, which uses Google Cloud Platform data centers. We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS
  • Authentication: Secure authentication using Firebase Authentication
  • Access Controls: Strict access controls and user permissions
  • Secure Storage: Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text

5. Your Privacy Rights

5.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access Your Data: View all data associated with your account
  • Modify Your Data: Update your account information and job records
  • Delete Your Data: Delete your entire account and all associated data
  • Export Your Data: Export your data in a portable format

5.2 Location Data Control

You can control location access:

  • Enable/disable location services in iOS Settings
  • Choose "While Using App" or "Always" permission
  • Stop job tracking at any time to stop location collection

5.3 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete, and the right to opt-out of sale. Note: We do not sell personal information.

5.4 European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under GDPR including the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and to object to processing.

6. Children's Privacy

ProfitApp is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time.

8. International Data Transfers

If you are located outside the United States, please be aware that information we collect will be transferred to and processed in the United States. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside your country of residence.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

10. App Store Privacy Details

For transparency, here is a detailed breakdown of data collection as required by Apple's App Store:

Data Linked to You

  • Contact Info: Email address
  • Location: Precise location (only during job tracking)
  • User Content: Job data, expenses, receipts, notes
  • Identifiers: User ID, device ID
  • Usage Data: App interactions, feature usage

Data Not Linked to You

  • Diagnostics: Crash logs, performance data (anonymized)

Data Used for Tracking

None. We do not use your data for tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Contact Us

Email: privacy@profitapp.com
Support: support@profitapp.com
Response Time: We will respond to privacy inquiries within 30 days.

Data Deletion Requests: To request deletion of your data, email privacy@profitapp.com with "Data Deletion Request" in the subject line.
